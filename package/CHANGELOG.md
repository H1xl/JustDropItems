# Changelog

## 1.0.0 — First release
- Drop items from your inventory, or give them to a teammate by dragging them onto a player in the scoreboard;
  given items fly to them as an icon.
- Choose the amount with the mouse wheel (or gamepad); the window shows Permanent, Ethereal and Temporary copies.
- Copies keep their kind: Temporary copies keep their decay, Ethereal copies vanish at the end of the stage.
- Equipment can be dropped and given (into an empty slot); used-up items can be given.
- Fair by default: only your own items, no lunar or void items, and each item can be rerolled by the Recycler or the Drifter
  only once. The host can change all of it.
- The Drifter's Tinker and Substandard Duplicator behave as in the base game with dropped and printed items.
- Chat lines about drops and gives in each player's language; optional `/drop` and `/give` chat commands.
- Gamepad controls.
- Every setting can be changed in game without a restart with Risk Of Options (optional), or in the config file.
